Walli Cases iPhone 18 Cases Launch Strategy

Prepared by BAD Marketing  ·  Amazon Department

Strategy

As the existing iPhone 17 cases are confirmed to be compatible with the corresponding iPhone 18 models, Walli can retain the existing products, ASINs, SKUs and UPCs for the iPhone 18 launch.

Recommendation: Keep the existing product listings. Launch the new positioning.

Rather than creating separate iPhone 18 listings or inventory, Walli can leverage the established ASINs to capture new demand generated by the iPhone 18 launch.

This approach allows Walli to create the visibility and momentum of a new iPhone 18 launch while directing that traffic into established ASINs that already have reviews, ranking and sales history.

Retain Existing ASINs

Continue selling through the existing iPhone 17 ASINs rather than creating separate iPhone 18 listings.

Retain:
  • Existing ASINs
  • Existing SKUs
  • Existing UPCs
  • Existing inventory
  • Reviews and ratings
  • Sales and ranking history

Creating new listings would unnecessarily split traffic and sales between identical products while giving up the history already established on the current ASINs.

The existing ASINs should instead be repositioned to clearly communicate compatibility with both the corresponding iPhone 17 and iPhone 18 models.

Prepare Inventory for Launch

Ensure sufficient inventory is available ahead of the iPhone 18 launch.

The existing inventory will now support both ongoing iPhone 17 demand and new iPhone 18 demand, with an expected increase in sales around the new device launch.

Inventory levels should be prepared accordingly to minimize stockout risk during the launch period.

Update Packaging

Continue using the existing packaging and apply a dual-compatibility sticker.

Recommended format:

Compatible with iPhone 17 Pro & iPhone 18 Pro

This ensures customers purchasing for iPhone 18 do not receive packaging labeled only for iPhone 17 and assume they received the wrong product.

For future production runs, update the packaging permanently to display both compatible models.

There is no need to maintain separate iPhone 17 and iPhone 18 packaging.

Update Existing Listings

Transition the existing product pages from iPhone 17-only positioning to iPhone 17 + iPhone 18 compatibility.

All listing updates should be coordinated to ensure clear and consistent compatibility messaging across the product pages.

Catalog Attributes

Update the Compatible Phone Models attribute across all applicable ASINs to include both corresponding generations.

Example:
CurrentiPhone 17 Pro Max
UpdatediPhone 17 Pro Max, iPhone 18 Pro Max

Verify that the updated compatibility attributes successfully publish across all applicable listings.

SEO & Copy

Complete iPhone 18 keyword research and update:

  • Titles
  • Bullets / Item Highlights
  • Backend search terms

The goal is to add iPhone 18 relevance without replacing established iPhone 17 relevance.

Example title direction:

Walli [Case Name] – Compatible with iPhone 17 Pro & iPhone 18 Pro – [Key Feature]

Compatibility Image

Update the existing compatibility image across every applicable listing to clearly communicate compatibility with both corresponding models.

Example:

Compatible with iPhone 17 Pro + iPhone 18 Pro

There is no need to introduce an additional iPhone 18-specific listing image. The existing compatibility asset should provide the visual confirmation of device compatibility, while the remaining image stack continues to focus on product benefits and differentiation.

Create the iPhone 18 Launch Moment

Although the underlying products are unchanged, the shopper-facing execution should be treated as a full iPhone 18 launch.

The core launch message across Amazon should be:

IPHONE 18 CASES ARE HERE

Walli should actively announce the new generation and provide iPhone 18 shoppers with a clear, dedicated shopping path.

Store Navigation & iPhone 18 Subpage

Maintain Walli's existing generation-based Store structure and add a dedicated iPhone 18 subpage.

The existing iPhone 17 page and navigation should remain unchanged.

The new iPhone 18 page should feature the same compatible ASINs currently available within the corresponding iPhone 17 collection, organized by iPhone 18 model.

Keeping the Store pages separate provides a cleaner shopping journey: shoppers can select their device generation and immediately access the relevant case selection, even though the iPhone 17 and iPhone 18 pages ultimately display the same products.

Homepage Launch Placement

Update the Store homepage with a prominent iPhone 18 launch asset.

Example direction:

iPhone 18 Cases Are Here — Find your new case.

Link directly to the new iPhone 18 subpage.

Product Selector Quiz

Update the existing homepage product selector to incorporate iPhone 18.

Shoppers should be able to select their iPhone 18 model and be routed to the corresponding compatible cases.

Existing iPhone 17 selections should remain unchanged.

Brand Story

Introduce an iPhone 18 launch asset within Brand Story to extend the launch message across Walli's broader Amazon catalog.

Example direction:

iPhone 18 Cases Are Here — Shop the Collection →

Link directly to the dedicated iPhone 18 Store subpage.

Drive iPhone 18 Discovery

Support the launch with dedicated advertising and creator activation built specifically around new iPhone 18 demand.

PPC Launch

Build dedicated iPhone 18 advertising coverage around:

  • Core iPhone 18 case terms
  • Model-specific terms
  • Relevant feature/style searches
  • Product targeting
  • Competitor targeting

Maintain existing iPhone 17 campaigns while monitoring how demand shifts between generations.

Scale iPhone 18 investment based on actual search volume and performance.

Sponsored Brands Video

Develop a dedicated iPhone 18 video ad rather than relying only on existing evergreen creative.

Example direction:

Got a New iPhone 18? Your Case Is Ready.

Run Sponsored Brands Video against priority iPhone 18 and model-specific searches during the launch period.

Creator Connections

Launch a dedicated Creator Connections campaign for iPhone 18 cases.

Prioritize creators producing content around:

  • iPhone 18
  • New-phone setup/accessories
  • Phone case recommendations
  • Fashion/style accessories
  • Everyday phone protection
Example angle:

Just upgraded to iPhone 18? Your Walli case is ready.

Launch early enough to generate creator coverage around the main iPhone 18 purchase window.

Monitor & Optimize

Closely monitor both launch performance and customer experience following release.

Launch Performance

Track:

  • iPhone 18 search visibility and ranking
  • Sessions
  • Conversion rate
  • PPC performance
  • Sponsored Brands Video performance
  • Creator Connections performance
  • iPhone 18 sales contribution
  • Existing iPhone 17 performance
  • Inventory coverage
Customer Experience

Monitor:

  • Return rate and reasons
  • Voice of the Customer
  • Reviews
  • Compatibility / fit complaints
  • "Wrong item received" feedback

Strengthen compatibility or packaging messaging if recurring confusion is identified.
